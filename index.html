The Framework 12 Laptop

Welcome to my page, I'm building this project to earn my Framework 12 Laptop through Hack Club Terra

The Framework 12 is an amazing laptop, here are some of the interesting and cool features about it

A little bonus about the Framework 12 laptop is that it's very lightweight and easy to carry during travelling or while going to work or even school!

Hardware specifications

Unlike regular soldered laptops, the Framework 12 actually gives you direct access to service the ram, SSD storage and the battery yourself, using the included screwdriver

  • Display and touchscreen: 12.2-inch 1920x1200 resolution screen with 360-degree hinge and with also stylus support
  • It's also upgrade friendly: You can also change the storage and memory, it also supports DDR5 RAM and M.2 NVMe SSD storage so you can easily upgrade memory down the road
  • Hardware privacy switches: they have built-in physical switches to instantly turn off the 1080p camera and dual microphones

Is the laptop budget-friendly?

Well the Framework 12 Laptop is one of the most accessible and great for your value laptops that are available

  • Low starting price: The DIY edition starts at around $499 to $600, making it far more affordable than regular repairable tech
  • In the long-term savings: Instead of buying a brand new laptop for $1000 every few years, when a part breaks in the Framework 12 laptop, you only pay small amounts to replace individual broken parts or upgrade the motherboard
  • Bring your own parts: With the DIY edition, you can bring your own ram, SSD storage and operating system to save even more money upfront

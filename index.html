The Framework 12 Laptop

Welcome to my page, I'm building this project to earn my Framework 12 Laptop through Hack Club Terra

The Framework 12 is an amazing laptop, here are some of the interesting and cool features about it

A little bonus about the Framework 12 laptop is that it's very lightweight and easy to carry during travelling or while going to work or even school!

Hardware specifications

Unlike regular soldered laptops, the Framework 12 actually gives you direct access to service the ram, SSD storage and the battery yourself, using the included screwdriver

  • Display and touchscreen: 12.2-inch 1920x1200 resolution screen with 360-degree hinge and with also stylus support
  • It's also upgrade friendly: You can also change the storage and memory, it also supports DDR5 RAM and M.2 NVMe SSD storage so you can easily upgrade memory down the road
  • Hardware privacy switches: they have built-in physical switches to instantly turn off the 1080p camera and dual microphones

Is the laptop budget-friendly?

Well the Framework 12 Laptop is one of the most accessible and great for your value laptops that are available

  • Low starting price: The DIY edition starts at around $499 to $600, making it far more affordable than regular repairable tech
  • In the long-term savings: Instead of buying a brand new laptop for $1000 every few years, when a part breaks in the Framework 12 laptop, you only pay small amounts to replace individual broken parts or upgrade the motherboard
  • Bring your own parts: With the DIY edition, you can bring your own ram, SSD storage and operating system to save even more money upfront

Is the Framework 12 Laptop built to last?

Unlike regular glued together laptops that end up in the garbage or landfills, when a single component fails, the Framework 12 Laptop is engineered from the ground up for modularity, repairability and longevity

It also has a customizable expansion card system

  • Recycled materials: Constructed using post-consumer recycled aluminum and plastics acrossthe top cover and lower bottom
  • Custom storage expansion: insert high-speed storage expansion cards directly into the side slots for extra space on demand
  • Open-source designs: Framewok publishes open-source 3d models so the community members can design custom module functions, which is actually really cool

Built in repairability and DIY spirit

  • The included screwdriver tool:Ships with a combination T5 Torx and spudger tool, which is the only tool required to fully disassemble the laptop
  • Scannable QR codes: Every internal part has a scannable QR code directing you directly to the official repair manuals and replacement part listings, which is very helpful
  • Clear labeling: Connectors, screws and component bays inside the bottom/chassis are explicitely labeled to eliminate guesswork during upgrades to make sure it's easy and not complicated

Modular Expansion cards

You can say goodbye to dongles, you can choose and swap out your own ports on the fly which is extremely cool and time efficient

  • USB-C, USB-A: For fast charging and connecting older accessories
  • HDMI and Displayport: Plug straight into monitors or projectors without adapters which could be extremely useful for teachers who don't wanna deal with the hastle of getting an adapter
  • MicroSD storage: Easily expand your file storage on the go in case you're presenting something in school or at work

Built for the planet

Traditional laptops are designed to be thrown away when a part breaks, The framework 12 lets you upgrade individual components instead of buying a whole new machine, drastically cutting down on electric waste and helping out the earth at the same time with being eco-friendly

Details on expected battery runtime and power management

Thermal Management and Cooling Architecture

A breakdown of the custom thermal subsystem designed to maintain maximum performance under sustained workloads

  • Dual Heatpipe Design: High-purity sintered copper heatpipes rapidly pull thermal energy directly away from the CPU die
  • Fluid Dynamic Bearing Fan: Ultra-quiet 75mm blower fan engineered for high static pressure with minimal acoustic resonance
  • Custom Vapor Chamber Option: Optional liquid-vapor phase transition chamber for high-wattage sustained performance modes
  • Exhaust Vent Geometry: Precisioned rear intake and exhaust grilles maximize volumetric airflow while preventing recirculation
  • Thermal Interface Material: Factory-applied phase-change thermal pad ensures long-term conductivity without pump-out degradation

Fan Speed Control Modes

Shows what the modes do

  • Silent mode: The fan turns off completely during light web browsing and text editing for zero noise, completely
  • Balanced mode: Dynamically ramps fan speed based on live internal temperature sensors to maintain quiet, steady cooling
  • Performance mode: Maximizes fan RPM to unlock full cpu power delivery during heavy compilation tasks

Wireless Connectivity and Network Architecture

Technical details regarding wireless connectivity, antenna placement and expansion card networking

    Frequently asked questions about the Frame 12 Laptop

    How do expansion cards work? Well expansion cards use a high-speed USB4 thunderbolt interface inside the side slots, you just push a quick release latch on the bottom of the laptop and slide out the current card then click a new one into place

    Can i buy a framework 12 without an OS? Yes you can, the DIY edition allows you to bring your own operating system, your own ram sticks and your own M.2 storage drive to save money on licensing and component costs

    What happens if a single key on the keyboard stops working? Well on standard laptops, replacing a keyboard often requires replacing the entire top aluminum shell, on the Framework 12, the keyboard deck unclips easily so you can swap just the keyboard input cover assembly in minutes
